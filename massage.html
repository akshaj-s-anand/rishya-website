Good to know

Questions before you book

What should I wear or bring?

Just yourself. You stay covered with linens throughout, and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

Can I add an enhancement?

Yes. Aromatherapy, hot stone, foot therapy or scalp therapy can be added to any 60 or 90 minute massage.

Do you offer a consultation for permanent makeup?

Yes, permanent makeup starts with a free consultation so we can plan shape and colour together.

Can I get a receipt for my insurance?

Yes, a receipt is available after every massage, and we can tell you about direct billing for your plan.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Text us as early as you can and we will find you a new time.
